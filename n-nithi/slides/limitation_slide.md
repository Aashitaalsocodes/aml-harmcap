\# Slide: Limitations (honest)

\- The dataset is synthetic (IBM AML, HI-Small and LI-Small). There is no real "innocent customer" label.

\- "Held innocent" is our definition: an account flagged in a window that sent no laundering transaction in that window.

\- The data spans only days to weeks, so windows are hours or single days.

\- Results show the method works; they are not a claim about a real bank.
